1 · Add your transaction data

Paste or upload your statements below. The tool reads debits (money going out) and ignores the rest. You can add several sources — bank, cards, UPI — and audit them together.

Where do I get each file? (bank statement, cards, UPI)

Bank account statement (best source): Net banking → Accounts → Statement → export CSV / Excel for the last 12 months. In the mobile app, look for “Download statement”. Prefer CSV; if you only get a PDF, open it and copy-paste the transaction rows into the box.

Credit / debit card statement: Card issuer portal or app → Statements → download CSV or PDF for each month. Many subscriptions sit on cards, not the bank account, so include these.

UPI AutoPay / mandate alerts: In your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) open Autopay / Mandates and the transaction history. You can paste the SMS/email debit alerts too — one line each, e.g. 12/09/2025 Netflix 649 debited.

12 months of history gives the most accurate cadence and price-hike detection. 3 months still works.

📲 Phone reminders

Add your renewal dates to your phone’s calendar. Your phone then alerts you ~3 days before each charge, so you can cancel in time. No account, no server — the reminders live in your own calendar and nothing is sent anywhere.

Tip: open the downloaded .ics file on your phone — it drops straight into your calendar and reminds you before every renewal.
