# Subscription Auditor — How & Where to Sell It

A step-by-step playbook for selling the audit as a **₹1,00,000 one-time** engagement. Written for a solo operator or a small team.

---

## The one-line pitch

> *"I run a private audit of every subscription and auto-debit your business pays for, find the ones bleeding money, and hand you a cancel-ready action plan. Your bank data never leaves your laptop. Most clients find far more in annual savings than the one-time fee."*

The product does the work; this guide is about **getting paid to point it at the right people.**

---

## 1 — Why this sells

- **Every business over-spends on subscriptions.** SaaS seats nobody uses, streaming/tools on the founder's card, trials that quietly converted, duplicate tools across teams. A 20–50 person company routinely wastes ₹2–8L/year.
- **The fee pays for itself.** If you surface ₹3L+ of annual waste, the ₹1L fee is a rounding error. Lead with the ROI, always.
- **The privacy angle removes the #1 objection.** Finance teams won't upload bank data to a random SaaS. "It never leaves your machine" is your unfair advantage — say it in the first two sentences.
- **It's a clean one-time transaction.** No subscription to sell (ironic, and a great line to use), no long sales cycle, no ongoing support burden.

---

## 2 — Who to sell to (in priority order)

| Buyer | Why they bite | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders / owners of 10–100 person companies** | Feel the burn personally, can say yes alone, hate waste | LinkedIn, founder WhatsApp/Slack groups, local startup meetups |
| **Finance managers / controllers** | Measured on cost control; this makes them look good | LinkedIn (title filter), CA/CFO networks |
| **Chartered Accountants & bookkeepers** | Resell to *their* client book — your best channel | CA associations, ICAI local chapters, referrals |
| **Agencies & IT services firms** | Pay for dozens of SaaS seats; high waste | Industry groups, their vendors |
| **Family offices / HNIs** | Audit personal + business subscriptions | Wealth-manager referrals |

**Start narrow.** Pick ONE segment (e.g. "D2C founders in [your city], 15–60 staff") and go deep before widening.

---

## 3 — Where to find them

1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator / search** — filter by title ("Founder", "Finance Manager", "Controller"), company size (11–200), and geography. This is your primary channel.
2. **Referrals from accountants** — one CA can introduce you to 20 clients. Offer them a 20–30% referral cut or a co-branded version.
3. **Local business & startup communities** — Chamber of Commerce, TiE, startup incubators, industry WhatsApp/Telegram groups, coworking spaces.
4. **Your own network first** — audit 2–3 friendly businesses free or cheap to get testimonials and real savings numbers. These numbers become your entire marketing.
5. **Content** — post a weekly "subscription waste teardown" on LinkedIn (anonymised). "I found ₹4.2L of dead SaaS in one 40-person company. Here's how." Inbound follows.

---

## 4 — The outreach message

Keep it short, lead with a number, make the ask tiny.

> **Subject: Found ₹3L+ of subscription waste for a company your size**
>
> Hi [Name] — I run a one-time subscription audit for businesses. I take your bank/card/UPI statements, find every recurring charge and the ones quietly wasting money, and give you a cancel-ready plan. **Your data never leaves your own laptop — I don't upload anything.**
>
> For a company your size I typically find ₹2–8L of annual savings. Fee is a flat ₹1L, one time.
>
> Worth a 20-minute call to show you how it works on sample data? No data needed from you for the demo.

**Follow up twice**, a few days apart. Most replies come on follow-up #1.

---

## 5 — The sales call (20–30 min)

1. **Qualify (2 min):** How many staff? Who owns the SaaS/subscriptions? Ever done an audit? → confirms there's waste to find.
2. **Demo on sample data (8 min):** Open the tool, click *Load sample data*, walk through Detect → Money drains → Dashboard → Cancel. Let them *see* the flags ("price up 30%", "duplicate category", "dead tool") and the cancellation assist. This sells itself.
3. **Hammer the privacy point (2 min):** "Notice there's no login and no upload. Open the network tab — zero requests. You can run it on an air-gapped laptop. Your statements stay with you."
4. **Anchor the ROI (3 min):** "Last [client type] I did, I found ₹X,XX,XXX a year. The fee is ₹1L, once. You keep the savings forever."
5. **Close (2 min):** "I can run yours this week. All I need is for you to export 12 months of statements — I'll guide you. Shall we book it?"

---

## 6 — Handling objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| *"₹1L is a lot."* | "It's one-time, and most clients find 3–8× that in annual savings. If I find less than ₹1L of waste, that itself is worth knowing — but I almost never do." |
| *"I'm not uploading bank data."* | "You're not. Nothing uploads — it runs on your own machine, offline if you like. I can even run it screen-share while you hold the file." |
| *"We already track our subscriptions."* | "Most teams track the big obvious ones. The money is in the forgotten UPI AutoPays, converted trials, and duplicate tools across teams. That's exactly what this surfaces." |
| *"Can you just do it cheaper / monthly?"* | "It's deliberately one-time — you don't need me every month. Re-run it yourself next quarter with the same tool." (Or offer a ₹25–40k quarterly re-audit retainer if they push.) |
| *"Send me info."* | "Happy to — but a 15-min screen-share shows it far better than a PDF. Tuesday or Thursday?" |

---

## 7 — Delivering the engagement

1. **Kickoff (15 min):** Send the client the tool file + `SETUP-GUIDE.md`. Walk them through exporting statements (bank + each card + UPI). You can do this screen-share so their data never even reaches you.
2. **Run the audit:** Either (a) you drive, screen-share, while they load their own file, or (b) they run it and share the exported JSON/PDF report with you. Both keep raw bank data on their side.
3. **Review session (30–45 min):** Walk the Dashboard and Money Drains with them. For each drain, agree keep/cancel. Use the Cancellation Assist to generate the emails and mandate-revocation steps live.
4. **Hand off:** Deliver the PDF report + the action plan + the drafted cancellation emails. Make the total annual savings the headline number on page one.
5. **Follow-up (1 week):** Check they actioned the cancellations and killed the mandates. This is where testimonials come from: *"[You] saved us ₹X a year in an afternoon."*

---

## 8 — Pricing & packaging options

- **Core: ₹1,00,000 one-time** — the full audit + review session + action plan.
- **Tiered by size** — ₹1L up to 50 staff, more above (scales with the number of sources and seats to analyse).
- **Savings-share alternative** — lower upfront (e.g. ₹25k) + 20% of year-one savings found. Higher ceiling; use when the buyer is fee-sensitive but confident there's waste.
- **Quarterly re-audit retainer** — ₹25–40k/quarter to re-run and catch new creep. Easy upsell after the first win.
- **White-label for accountants** — license the tool + method to a CA firm for their client book; revenue share or flat license.

---

## 9 — Scaling past one-at-a-time

1. **Testimonials with real rupee numbers** are your entire marketing engine. Collect one from every client.
2. **Build the accountant channel** — each CA is a multiplier. Give them a referral cut and a one-pager they can forward.
3. **Productise the demo** — a 3-minute screen-recording of the sample-data walkthrough, posted everywhere, does outreach while you sleep.
4. **Niche down and dominate** — become "the subscription audit person for [dentists / law firms / D2C brands]". Specific beats generic.
5. **Train a junior** to run the delivery calls once the pitch is repeatable; you focus on sales and the accountant channel.

---

## 10 — First-30-days action plan

- **Week 1:** Audit 2 friendly businesses free. Record the savings numbers and get 2 testimonials.
- **Week 2:** Write your outreach message, build a 50-name list in one niche, record the 3-min demo video.
- **Week 3:** Send 50 outreach messages + 2 follow-ups. Book demos. Ask your network for 3 accountant intros.
- **Week 4:** Run demos, close your first 1–2 paid audits, deliver, and collect testimonials. Reinvest into a bigger list.

**Goal:** one paying client in 30 days pays for the next quarter of selling. The product is built — now it's just reps.
